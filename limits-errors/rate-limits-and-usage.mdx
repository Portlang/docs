---
title: "Rate Limits & Usage"
---

To ensure system stability and support production-ready workflows, HS Ping enforces a unified rate limit of **1,000 lookups / minute** across all tiers.

This rate limit applies immediately to your account, including during your initial 100 trial lookup period.

## One-time Free Quota

All new accounts receive **100 trial lookups**. 

This is a **one-time, non-recurring credit** applied immediately upon selecting a subscription tier and adding your payment details.

Your card will not be charged immediately. Once the 100 trial lookups are exhausted, your chosen subscription tier will automatically activate and begin its normal monthly billing cycle.
